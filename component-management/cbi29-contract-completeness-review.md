Date: 2026-08-03

Scope: absence review of the CBI29 fanned-out child-Port activation contract, separate from
conformance review.

## Findings and dispositions

1. **CBI22, CBI27, and CBI28 already form the activation path.** Disposition: established. Both
   roots activate the complete wide position through the existing child entry point; no runtime,
   planning, authority, or portable surface was added.
2. **The child wrapper hid structural preparation refusals.** Disposition: corrected in both roots.
   `membership-not-resolved`, `member-scope-required`, and `scope-not-distinct` reached the child as
   `PreparationUnavailable`, but the wrapper replaced them with `child-establishment-refused`.
   Plan and preparation classifications now survive; actual provider/runtime establishment failures
   retain the child-level code.
3. **Port containment remains earlier than authority.** Disposition: pinned. An attachment naming
   another Port is refused with no child admission or provider effect. Every accepted member comes
   from the one position the generation placed in the attached Port.
4. **The two scope concepts do not collapse.** Disposition: pinned. The child occupies one restart
   scope distinct from its parent while each member owns a distinct portable binding scope.
5. **Authority and Release remain activation-wide barriers.** Disposition: pinned. Authority is
   admitted per member before provider contact; denial produces no partial child, and one member not
   reaching Ready releases none of the child members.
6. **The parent is observationally unchanged for every outcome.** Disposition: pinned across success,
   structural refusal, authority denial, provider failure, and Port mismatch. Its members stay
   released and no parent provider is contacted by the child attempt.
7. **This closes CBI28 finding 8 without widening its other bounds.** Disposition: recorded. The
   evidence covers one wide position in one runtime-open child Port. It does not span Ports, fill
   optional capacity, run a degraded set, infer an attachment, or resolve Decisions 12 through 16.
8. **Independent agreement is still bounded evidence.** Disposition: retained. Shared vectors and
   native named properties force the composition and its negative space in both roots, but do not
   establish production distribution or general Provider Set completeness.

## Result

The CBI29 contract is complete for activating one fanned-out position inside one child Port over the
fake runtime. The combination needs no new activation semantics. Its only implementation correction
is preserving structural preparation classifications through the child wrapper. CBI28's child-Port
bound is closed; its optional-capacity, degraded-service, and cross-scope decisions remain open.
